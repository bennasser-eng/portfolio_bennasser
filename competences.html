A Survey of Large Language Models (Zhao et al., 2023, updated March 2026)

Comprehensive Survey (140+ pages)

In-depth study of the foundational architectures, pre-training objectives, adaptation techniques, and evaluation protocols governing modern LLMs.

  • Evolution: Evolution process of Language Modeling (LM) and Large Language Models (LLMs) in the context of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Generative AI (GAI).
  • Pre-training & Architecture: Transformer-based backbones, tokenization, positional encodings, scaling laws, and distributed training paradigms.
  • Adaptation: Instruction tuning, alignment methodologies (RLHF, PPO, DPO, ORPO) and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (Adapters, LoRA, QLoRA).
  • Inference & Utilization: In-context learning, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting, Graph-of-Thought (GoT) and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and tool use.
  • Evaluation & Safety: Benchmarking reasoning abilities, hallucinations mitigation, and reliability metrics.
